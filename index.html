Changelog — DocConfig Most recent first
Feature
Template Revision Control & Retirement — Templates now carry full version history with mandatory change reasons on every edit. Admins can retire templates to a read-only archive via a two-field confirmation flow (reason + notes required, Confirm button stays locked until both are filled). Full reverse-timeline visible in sidebar History view.
Jun 8, 2026
New
Training Packages module — Create packages by selecting from a library of 5 pre-built training plans. Package builder shows live summary of total SOPs covered and estimated training hours. Progress tracking (done/total), media attachments, and per-assignee records.
Jun 8, 2026
New
Quality Alert & Safety Alert libraries — Dedicated tabs for QA and SA records. Priority badges (Critical/High/Medium/Low), status tracking (Open/Monitoring/Closed), linked SOPs and specs, media attachments. Standardised New QA and New SA forms with full CAPA fields.
Jun 7, 2026
Feature
Photos & Videos in SOPs — Drag-and-drop media upload on New SOP form accepts .jpg, .png, .mp4, .mov. Videos render as dark navy thumbnail with play icon. Media count shown in SOP library table with photo/video indicators.
Jun 7, 2026
Feature
Approval Workflows, Version History, Access & Roles — Visual step pipeline editor for approval workflows. Reverse-timeline version history per document. 8-person role roster with Admin / Approver / Author / Viewer badges.

New
DocConfig v1 — Initial Release — SOP library, Engineering Drawings library, Reference Specifications across 5 named libraries, SOP Templates with section builder, tag and status filters, spec linker, and full ClearPath / Spectrum Killian branding system.
